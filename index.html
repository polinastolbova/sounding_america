A place to listen

GENERATIVE AUDIO · STEREO SPACE

This is not an audiobook. There are no readings layered over the sound. Each space is an independent sonic environment: a field that breathes, shifts, and produces events over time.

Move through the chapters at your own pace. The route is a suggestion, not a rule. You can pause, return, or listen to one place for as long as you like. Text and controls remain available throughout.

Listen with attention

AN INVITATION, NOT AN INSTRUCTION

Notice what stays constant and what suddenly appears. Listen for low vibrations, small bright details, distance, repetition, and the spaces between events.

For the best experience, use headphones at a comfortable volume. The embedded player below runs the original Csound project. Select the matching .csd file in its File menu to hear that composition.
